## The Challenge

We were losing an excessive amount of time repeatedly teaching everything our department does to new employees and interns. The process was manual, tedious, and highly inefficient, taking valuable time away from our core operations.

## The Solution

To solve this, I designed and developed a comprehensive **Gamified Onboarding Application**. 

Built entirely as a standalone web application, it simulates a game environment where new employees and interns can independently learn our systems, policies, and processes through interactive modules, roleplay simulators, and progress trackers.

## Impact

By converting a manual training process into a self-paced, gamified experience, we standardized the knowledge transfer and significantly reduced the time senior staff spent on basic training—freeing up the department to focus on higher-value tasks.
