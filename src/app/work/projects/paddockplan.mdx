# PaddockPlan

**Reduced manual SLA follow-up by ~70% across 800+ annual cases.**

## Context
The organization was facing a high volume of manual case management, leading to delayed SLA tracking and inconsistent data reporting. Stakeholders lacked real-time visibility into operational bottlenecks, and staff spent excessive hours following up on individual requests instead of focusing on high-value tasks.

## Approach
Built a SaaS application on Vercel utilizing Antigravity, Copilot Studio, and Power Automate to streamline operations and enhance automated workflows.
- **Power Automate**: Cloud flows to trigger immediate SLA-threshold alerts and compile automated KPI digests.
- **Copilot Studio / AI Agents**: Designed and rolled out intelligent agents to assist with data QA, summarization, and template generation.
- **Vercel & Antigravity**: For modern, fast, and scalable deployment of the web interfaces.

## What I learned
This project underscored the incredible value of combining standard RPA (Power Automate) with generative AI (Copilot Studio). While Power Automate handled the strict rules-based SLA tracking effortlessly, integrating LLMs allowed us to tackle unstructured data (like summarizing case threads and QAing data entries) which previously required human intervention. The key challenge was ensuring responsible AI adoption—implementing strict validation routines before any AI-generated data was shared with external stakeholders.
